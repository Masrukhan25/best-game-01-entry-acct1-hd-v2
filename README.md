An original HD neon space shooter, built for the [best-game-01 bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/best-game-01) on make.fun. All art and sound are generated in code — no image or audio files, no network requests.

## The game

You are the last light of the Void. Hold the line through **21 waves** of the swarm, with **10 enemy kinds** (drifters, darters, weavers, spinners, tanks, splitters, mites, snipers, chargers, orbiters), **3 mini-bosses** (Helix Warden, Nova Herald, Gravity Maw) and the final boss **VOID SOVEREIGN** in 3 phases of bullet-hell.

- **Weapons & upgrades:** after waves 3, 6, 9, 12, 15 and 18, pick 1 of 3 upgrades — damage, fire rate, multishot, piercing rounds, hull plating, drones, magnet, bombs, thrusters, nano-weave.
- **Power-ups:** rapid fire, spread shot, shield, wingman drone, nova bomb, overdrive.
- **Wingman drone:** an orbiting ally that hunts the nearest enemy on its own.
- **Combo:** chain kills to raise the multiplier up to x8; it decays if you stop scoring.
- **Feel:** parallax nebula, a ringed planet, three star layers, glow sprites, particles, screen shake, hit flashes, floating score text, and a synthesized WebAudio soundtrack (132 BPM) with sound effects.

## How to play

- **WASD / Arrow keys** — fly
- **Mouse** — aim, **Click / Space** — fire
- **Shift / Right-click** — nova bomb (clears bullets, hurts everything)
- **P** — pause, **M** — mute, **R** — restart
- Clear a wave, choose an upgrade, survive all 21 waves to win.

## Play it

Serve `game/` with any static file server and open `game/index.html` in desktop Chrome. No build step, no network.
